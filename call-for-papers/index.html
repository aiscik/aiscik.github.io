iTracks

Tracks describe the form of a contribution, not the discipline it comes from. For example, conceptual, historical, and qualitative work belongs in Research when it reports a completed investigation; the Perspectives track is for arguments, whatever methods the author works with. If a submission could plausibly sit in two tracks, pick the one you find most fitting — we will move it if the reviewers think it belongs elsewhere.
